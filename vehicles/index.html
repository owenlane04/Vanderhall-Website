Vehicles

Two three-wheel gas roadsters, one three-wheel electric autocycle, and one electric off-road UTV, hand-built in Provo, Utah. Venice and Carmel are past models.

Brawley

Quad-motor electric off-road UTV with a seating capacity of four. Published output spans 283 to 404 hp with 488 lb-ft of torque, up to 140 mi of range, and 21 in of suspension travel.

Explore Brawley →

Santarosa

Three-wheel electric autocycle. Twin motors drive the front wheels and produce 180 hp and 216 lb-ft, with a published standard range of 150 mi.

Explore Santarosa →

Carmel

Past model

Open two-seat gas roadster on three wheels. The photographs show red bodywork, a leather-wrapped three-spoke wheel, four analog gauges, and tan contrast-stitched seats.

Venice

Past model
