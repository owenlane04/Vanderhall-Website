Safety notice

Always wear a helmet when operating a Vanderhall vehicle. Read the operator's manual and all safety warnings. Complete a safety training course and practice to become skilled with how to drive the vehicle. Local laws may require special licensing to operate the vehicle.

POWERTRAIN

Quad-motor 4WD

Brawley uses four electric motors to drive all four wheels. Verified output spans 283 to 404 hp with 488 lb-ft of torque.

RANGE

Up to 140 mi

Published Brawley range is up to 140 mi. The verified figure applies to the Brawley product described in the current source material.

DRIVE MODES

Five ways to direct four motors

Published modes are 4x2, 4x4, eCrawl, eCrab, and eSteer. Each mode name is carried directly from Vanderhall's Brawley feature material.

SUSPENSION

21 in of travel

Published suspension travel is 21 in. The measurement remains text in the page and is also included in the specification table.

CAPABILITY

1,500 lb towing

Four-seat capacity
