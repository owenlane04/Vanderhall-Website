What Is a Side-by-Side? Experience the Future with the Vanderhall Brawley

What defines a side-by-side, and how the all-electric Vanderhall Brawley takes capability, comfort, and control into a new era.

When someone asks what is a side-by-side, they’re referring to a purpose-built, off-road vehicle designed for adventure. In essence, it’s a rugged machine engineered to carry more than one passenger across challenging terrain, combining capability, comfort, and control in equal measure.

At Vanderhall USA, we’ve reimagined this definition entirely. Our all-electric vehicles push the boundaries of what a side-by-side can be, blending precision engineering with sustainable performance. Let’s explore how these vehicles came to define modern adventure and how the electric Vanderhall Brawley takes it to the next level.

What Is a Side-by-Side Vehicle?

Also known as a UTV (Utility Task Vehicle) or SxS, a side-by-side is a compact, open-air vehicle built for performance and versatility. Featuring side-by-side seating for two or more passengers, a steering wheel, seat belts, and foot pedals, it combines the feel of a traditional automobile with the agility of an off-road machine.

Designed for rugged environments, from mountain trails to desert dunes, side-by-sides typically include roll cages, advanced suspension systems, and cargo beds for hauling gear. They strike the ideal balance between recreation and function, equally suited to outdoor exploration or heavy-duty work.

The Difference Between ATVs and Side-by-Sides

The distinction between an ATV and a side-by-side lies in both design and purpose. ATVs, or All-Terrain Vehicles, are built for single riders and use handlebar steering. Side-by-sides, on the other hand, offer enclosed seating, greater safety features, and a steering wheel for refined control.

Their versatility is another defining trait. UTVs can tackle trail riding, cargo hauling, towing, or even worksite transport. With larger frames and higher payload and towing capacities, they outperform standard ATVs in both endurance and capability.

Why Off-Roaders Love Side-by-Sides

Those who’ve experienced a side-by-side firsthand know it’s one of the most thrilling ways to connect with nature. Here’s why:

  • Power and Performance: Robust drivetrains deliver remarkable torque and acceleration.
  • Safety: Roll cages, seat belts, and reinforced frames enhance protection.
  • Comfort: Ergonomic seating and spacious cabins allow for long rides with ease.
  • Utility: Ample cargo space makes it easy to bring along tools, supplies, or gear.
  • Agility: Designed for tight trails, steep climbs, and unpredictable terrain.

They’re designed to make every adventure memorable, whether that’s climbing dunes, crossing creeks, or heading deep into forest trails.

The Evolution of the Side-by-Side

Early side-by-sides were primarily utilitarian, built for farms and job sites with durability and hauling capacity in mind. As the category evolved, brands like Polaris, Can-Am, and Yamaha refined the formula for recreation and performance.

Now, the future has arrived. With the demand for cleaner energy, quieter operation, and advanced technology, Vanderhall has ushered in a new era of the electric side-by-side.

Why Go Electric for Off-Road Adventures?

Electric mobility is transforming every aspect of transportation, and off-roading is no exception. The benefits speak for themselves:

  • Instant Torque: Immediate power delivery for responsive climbing and maneuvering.
  • Quiet Operation: Immerse yourself in the natural world without engine noise.
  • Reduced Maintenance: Fewer moving parts mean less upkeep and greater reliability.
  • Sustainability: Zero emissions ensure the trails you love remain pristine.

Choosing the Right Side-by-Side for You

Before investing in a side-by-side, consider your intended use and environment:

  • Seating Configuration: Two- or four-seat setups for solo or shared adventures.
  • Cargo and Towing Needs: Match capacity to your lifestyle and terrain.
  • Terrain Type: Trail, desert, or mountain; each demands different specs.
  • Comfort Features: Options like enclosed cabins and climate control elevate the ride.
