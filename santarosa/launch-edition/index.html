Be Among the First.

The Vanderhall Santarosa Launch Edition

United States only

Register your interest

A new chapter is about to begin.

The Santarosa Launch Edition will be limited to just 50 individually numbered vehicles. Each will be built with exclusive features available only on this inaugural edition.

Launch Edition highlights

  • Individually numbered Launch Edition badging, 1 of 50
  • Exclusive Launch Edition specialty options package
  • 40 kWh battery*
  • Carbon-fiber dashboard
  • Carbon-fiber glovebox
  • Carbon-fiber center console
  • Carbon-fiber door-jamb trim
  • Carbon-fiber front spoiler
  • Carbon-fiber ducktail
  • Additional exclusive Launch Edition enhancements

More details will be announced as we get closer to launch.

Expected deliveries

Initial customer deliveries are expected to begin during the fourth quarter of 2026.

Reservation priority

Reservation opportunities will be offered in this order:

  1. Existing Santarosa reservation holders
  2. Authorized Vanderhall dealers
  3. Public reservations, anticipated by the end of August 2026 and subject to availability
